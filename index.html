FAQ

More? Ask on WhatsApp.

Cash on Delivery?

Yes. You pay when it arrives.

When will it arrive?

About 20 days from your order.

iPhone and Android?

Both. Type-C phones (most Androids, iPhone 15+) plug in with the converter in the box. Lightning iPhones need Apple’s camera adapter.

Is the SD card included?

Yes, already inside the camera.

How do I charge it?

Any USB charger, with the cable in the box.

Does it shoot video?

Yes. KeyCam converts clips so they play on your phone.

Can I turn off the date?

The camera always stamps it. The app can restyle it or crop it out.
