FAQ

Good questions.

Something not here? Message us and a person will answer.

Can I pay Cash on Delivery?

Yes. Every order is Cash on Delivery. You pay when the camera reaches you, and we call before it ships to confirm your address and colour.

When will it arrive?

Glimp is on pre-order. Orders ship in about 20 days, and we’ll call you when yours is on its way.

Does it work with iPhone and Android?

Yes. The KeyCam app is for both. Phones with a Type-C port (most Androids, iPhone 15 and later) plug in with the converter in the box. Older iPhones with Lightning need Apple’s Lightning to USB camera adapter, or you can use any micro SD card reader.

Is the SD card really included?

Really. It comes already inside the camera, so you can shoot the moment it’s out of the box.

How do I charge it?

With the cable in the box, from any USB charger or laptop. It’s the same Type-C port you use to move photos.

Does it shoot video?

Yes, short clips. The camera saves them in an old format your phone won’t play, so KeyCam converts them on import. They play like any other video.

Can I turn off the date stamp?

The camera always stamps the date, which is half the charm. In the app you can restyle it or crop it out of a layout.
